1. Executive Purpose & Mission Alignment

The objective of this request is to establish a dedicated supervisory tier for civilian Control Point Operators (CPOs) to professionalize oversight, enhance facility security, and provide necessary career progression within the Utah Department of Corrections (UDC). By implementing the Control Point Operator II (CPO II) position, the Department will maximize the value, skills, and long-term retention of CPOs while mitigating legal liability through specialized oversight of civilian-staffed security posts.

This initiative serves as the primary strategic mechanism to stabilize our civilian workforce, reduce turnover, and align departmental operations with modern correctional standards across the Utah State Correctional Facility (USCF) and Central Utah Correctional Facility (CUCF).

2. CPO II "Rover" Supervisory Model

The CPO II will operate under a mobile "Rover" supervision model, providing active technical, administrative, and psychological support across assigned control posts without remaining static at a single station.

High-Level Operations
Initiating facility counts, contacting county jails to coordinate transfers, logging discrepancies, and tracking UMC transports.
Post Standardization
Auditing posts to assemble standardized Post Binders (IMS emergency protocols & unusual occurrences).
Administrative Triage
Managing inmate complaints, reviewing daily logs, approving timesheets, and overseeing leave coordination.
Technical Systems
Expert O-Track lookup assistance for housing officers and systems documentation via Vantage and BlueTeam.

5. Proposed 6-Stage Merit Promotional Framework

To eliminate political favoritism and ensure only the most qualified, competent leaders advance into supervisory roles, the promotional process will follow a structured 6-point evaluation framework aligned with UDC Policy AF19 (Staff Promotional Process):

Stage 1
Application & Point-Based Resume
Formal application and weighted scoring of professional background, experience, and qualifications.
Stage 2
Peer & Supervisor Reviews
Feedback from housing officers, sergeants, and lieutenants evaluating character and rapport.
Stage 3
Open-Book Written Examination
Evaluates policy navigation and manual application across UDC manuals, udc.utah.gov, UDCCONNECT and post orders. Open-book measures competency, not memorization.
Stage 4
Reliability Audit
Formal administrative review of attendance, punctuality, leave history, and disciplinary status per AF19 guidelines.
Stage 5
Practical Scenario Assessment
Evaluates real-time decision making during simulated control room emergencies and HMI/IMS activations.
Stage 6
Structured Panel Interview
Formal interview board conducted in full compliance with UDC Policy AF19 guidelines.
